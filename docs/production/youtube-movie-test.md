# AFTERLIGHT Vertical Slice — YouTube Movie Test

Purpose: the 8-minute slice must work as **recorded footage**, not only as a played game.

Capture with cine / HUD-free mode (**H**). No debug overlay. No Trust numbers. No “Press E to Talk” unless a viewer would accept it as a subtitle beat — prefer world prompting (Maya looking at him, a hand on a door).

This is not Photo Mode. It is a take.

## 2. Viewer questions (must all be yes)

- Does this look like a directed scene if you do not know it is Unreal?
- Does Maya ever stand like an NPC waiting for overlap?
- Do camera blends feel like operators, not game-camera snaps?
- Would a viewer who never pressed WASD still follow the story?
- Is the choice visible without a UI recap?
- Does the recording change the meaning of the mug beat?
- Do you want to know what “the rest” is?
- Do you want to know whether Eli should want her in the room?

If the take only works with debug overlay on, it is not a movie test.

---

## 3. Common game-tells to hunt

- Interaction prompts lingering into close-ups
- Companion rubber-band
- Footslide
- Double shadows / light pop when changing registers
- Dialogue UI over Intimate eyes
- Player walking through Maya
- Hide volume invisible until fail
- Sequence start/stop pop
- Title over gameplay instead of black

---

## 4. Relationship visibility test

Record **two takes**, same camera path as much as possible:

1. Choice **Walk.**
2. Choice **You talk like I belong to you.**

A viewer who does not know the axes should still notice **at least two** of: distance, touch during the sweep, use of the name, whether she sits, whether the joke completes, how she reaches for the tin.

If only follow-distance changes, add the sleeve/palm and the joke before calling texture done.

---

## 5. Passive-viewing pacing

Play the take back **without touching the mouse**.

- Walk-and-talk must still point the body generally through the Cut (Maya leads; player can drift but the take should be recorded with disciplined movement).
- Do not leave dead air waiting for an overlap the viewer cannot see. If a trigger is late, Maya should start Talk / Cover.
- Recording at ~18s is short enough that Locked does not feel like “cutscene mode” if the rest is playable.

---

## 6. Capture notes

- Resolution: whatever the laptop can hold without hitching; hitches read as amateur.
- Cine mode **H** before rolling.
- Temp VO is acceptable for blocking takes; hero VO before any public upload.
- One cut allowed: hide fail-soft. Prefer zero.

---

## 7. Pass / fail for the slice as a trailer source

**Pass:** someone who has never heard of AFTERLIGHT watches the file and asks who Maya is to him.

**Fail:** someone says “cool Unreal scene” or “what do I fight next.”
