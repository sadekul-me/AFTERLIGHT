# AFTERLIGHT Vertical Slice — Screenplay

**Afterlight Window**  
Slice 01 · ~8:15 · Production draft  
Aligned to `vertical-slice-story-bible.md` and `vertical-slice-beats.md`.

Conventions:

- `INT./EXT.` headings are space names, not film stages.
- `[Register | Input]` at the start of a beat.
- `PLAYER` = control/interaction, not a spoken character.
- `IF FOLLOW` / `IF QUESTION` = texture only. Same rooms, same reveal.
- Camera notes only when they matter.
- Dialogue has already been through a pass: if a line explained the world, it was cut.

Characters:

**ELI** — unverified name. He does not introduce himself.  
**MAYA** — she does.

---

## B01 — WAKE

**EXT. UNDERDECK 9 — NIGHT**  
**[Explore | Constrained → Full]**

Rain sheets off the transit deck above in hard curtains. The underdeck is a service throat: pipes, lagging, sodium lamps in a line, puddles the color of oil.

A man lies against a concrete rib. Mid-thirties. Wet through. No wound that matters. A cheap clip on his collar holds nothing.

He opens his eyes. He looks at his hands as if they might be someone else’s.

PLAYER: look. After a few seconds, Full — stand, walk.

He stands too fast. The world tilts. He catches the rib. Spits rain.

Down the throat, a **Witness post** sits on the wall: a small civic box, lens dark, LED dead.

He can walk to it. He does not have to.

No music.

Footsteps in water. Not trying to hide.

---

## B02 — CONTACT

**EXT. UNDERDECK 9 — NIGHT**  
**[Explore → Dialogue | Full → Constrained]**

MAYA is already wet. Coat too thin. Hair stuck to her cheek. She looks like she has been walking the stacks for hours and is angry about the weather, not about destiny.

She sees him standing. Something in her face goes through relief and irritation in the same second.

MAYA  
Don’t stand up fast.

He is already standing.

ELI  
I heard you.

PLAYER: when close and facing, **Talk**.

Camera blends to Dialogue, over Maya’s shoulder onto him, then a reverse so we can read her. No snap.

She looks at his eyes too long. Checklist.

MAYA  
How many fingers.

She does not hold fingers up.

ELI  
You’re going to have to be more specific.

A breath. Almost a smile. She kills it.

MAYA  
Eli.

He does not take the name. He looks at her mouth, then past her, at the rain curtain.

MAYA  
Okay.  
We can do it the long way.

She glances at the Witness post. The LED is still dead.

MAYA  
We have to move. If you stay here they will write you down, and I will not get you out of that.

---

## B03 — CHOICE

**EXT. UNDERDECK 9 — NIGHT**  
**[Dialogue | Constrained]**

PLAYER — one choice. Both reasonable.

**1. Walk.**  
*(He starts toward the stair.)*

**2. “You talk like I belong to you.”**

---

### IF FOLLOW

He walks. She has to catch up half a step, then she is ahead of him, because that is how she walks when she is allowed to hope.

She looks back once. Uses the name like a private thing:

MAYA  
(quietly)  
Eli.

Camera may hold Intimate on that look for half a second. Then Explore. Full.

Trust +0.25. Close follow.

---

### IF QUESTION

ELI  
You talk like I belong to you.

MAYA  
I don’t.  
That’s the problem.

She does not use the name again. She waits until he moves, then takes the off-shoulder.

Explore. Full.

Suspicion +0.65. Far follow.

---

They climb a short wet stair. Rain becomes steam and shuttered light.

## B04 — LANTERN CUT

**EXT. LANTERN CUT — NIGHT**  
**[Explore | Full]**

A pedestrian cut between stacked housing. Afterlight quiet: not silence, just a city that has been told to go indoors. One kiosk still open. Broth steam. Plastic stools chained to the floor. A civic clock:

**AFTERLIGHT 02:17**

A laminated Helion notice, rain-warped:

**AFTERLIGHT HOURS — REDUCE GRID — WITNESS COVERAGE VARIABLE**

Someone has underlined VARIABLE in ballpoint.

Walk-and-talk. Player remains in Full. Maya’s lines should be skippable by distance but she will wait at the next corner.

MAYA  
Don’t look at the posts.  
They like faces.

ELI  
That one was dead.

MAYA  
They don’t stay dead because you asked.

If he looks at a live-looking lens anyway, she steps into his eyeline. No lecture.

MAYA  
I said don’t.

A child’s toy under a stool, wet. Neither of them picks it up.

ELI  
You going to tell me where.

MAYA  
A dry room.  
Then you can be difficult sitting down.

IF FOLLOW: she says it almost fond.  
IF QUESTION: it comes out flatter.

Distant hum. A white circle of light slides over a far shutter.

MAYA  
Lantern.  
Come on.

## B05 — SWEEP

**EXT. LANTERN CUT — KIOSK RECESS — NIGHT**  
**[Threat | Constrained]**

The **Helion lantern-drone** is slow and civic. A spotlight. A polite speaker.

DRONE (recorded, pleasant)  
Afterlight advisory. Please remain in registered shelter. This is a courtesy pass.

It does not sound like a hunter. That is worse.

PLAYER: enter the kiosk recess / shutter gap. Prompt **Cover** only if still in the open.

Maya is already counting under her breath, eyes on the light, not on him.

MAYA  
Four. They turn at four.

### IF FOLLOW

She takes his sleeve and pulls him into steam and plastic. The grab is not romantic. It is practiced.

### IF QUESTION

She puts an open hand toward his chest and does not touch him.

MAYA  
Down.

They wait. Drip. Broth. The spotlight crosses the chained stools and does not care about the toy.

Maya’s mouth finishes the count. She has done this enough times that her body knows it before her face does.

The drone turns at four, as she said. It leaves, still polite.

MAYA  
(soft)  
Okay.

Explore. Full.

If the player is late and the light grazes them: she yanks or hisses; the drone does not open fire; they still proceed. No death.

## B07 — QUIET

**INT. PUMP HOUSE 12 — NIGHT**  
**[Explore → Intimate | Full → Constrained → Scripted hold → Full]**

A decommissioned flood-control room. Gauges. A pump that still ticks when the building settles. Someone has been living in the corners: two mugs, a coat on a hook in his size, a towel, a work lamp.

Maya hits the lamp with her elbow. Warm light.

She finds the towel without looking. She is going to dry his hair and then she doesn’t — too intimate, too soon. She puts the towel in his hand instead.

MAYA  
You’re dripping on my floor.

ELI  
Your floor.

MAYA  
Tonight.

She pours from a thermos into a mug. Hands it over automatically — **left**.

He switches it to the right without thinking, then looks at what he did.

ELI  
Don’t know which one is mine.

MAYA  
Right.  
Sorry.

Intimate. Hold. Let this land. Then they can breathe.

### IF FOLLOW

She sits on the floor near his knees, not in a chair. Dries her own hair with the corner of the towel. The leftover humor arrives.

MAYA  
You used to hate that tick.  
Said it sounded like a cheap dishwasher.

A small sound. Not quite a laugh.

ELI  
Did it?

MAYA  
It sounded like a pump.

### IF QUESTION

She stays standing. Dries her own hair. The joke starts and dies in her mouth.

MAYA  
Drink it before it tastes like the pipe.

He drinks. It tastes like the pipe anyway.

PLAYER: look around. Optional **Inspect** the face-down photo on the gauge shelf. If flipped: the two of them. She is looking at the camera. He is not. No dialogue required.

The tin is in a drawer that does not belong on a pump schematic. He will see it if he looks. He is meant to look.

## B08 — TIN

**INT. PUMP HOUSE 12 — NIGHT**  
**[Reveal | Full → Constrained]**

PLAYER: **Inspect** tin.

A cheap metal tin. On the lid, in his handwriting, small:

**FOR WHEN IT TAKES**

### IF FOLLOW

MAYA  
Don’t—

She stops. Shame. She lets him.

### IF QUESTION

MAYA  
Eli—

Too fast. Her hand starts toward it. She aborts.

Inside: a personal slate, cracked, and a clip that matches the empty clip on his collar.

---

## B09 — WARNING

**INT. PUMP HOUSE 12 — NIGHT**  
**[Cinematic → Intimate | Locked → Scripted → Constrained]**

PLAYER: Locked. Level Sequence on the slate. His face as a still — the same man, drier, slept, looking into a lens he set up himself. Audio is the performance.

RECORDING (ELI)  
If this played, it took.

A breath. He hates speeches. He is making one anyway.

RECORDING (ELI)  
Maya will come. Let her. She’ll keep you alive.

Beat.

RECORDING (ELI)  
Do not help her with the rest.

Beat.

RECORDING (ELI)  
She’ll call it taking care of you.  
I called it that too.

Longer beat. He almost stops the recording. He doesn’t.

RECORDING (ELI)  
If you still want her in the room after this sentence,  
you are not finished becoming me.

Click.

Sequence releases. Intimate on Maya. She has been watching him, not the slate.

Silence. Let it be too long.

MAYA  
He sounds so sure.

ELI  
Was he?

MAYA  
No.

## B10 — AFTERLIGHT

**INT. PUMP HOUSE 12 — NIGHT**  
**[Reveal | Scripted → Locked]**

Through the door seam, the Witness LED on the Cut wall ticks from dead to live. White. Writing.

Maya looks at the door. Then at him. She does not ask him to come. She does not say his name.

He does not answer.

Rain finds a crack and starts again, very small.

Black.

**AFTERLIGHT**

No tagline.

## Dialogue cut list (pass notes)

Removed vs earlier impulses:

- No “Where am I.”
- No “I don’t remember anything.”
- No explanation of Helion as a conspiracy.
- No “trust me.”
- No “I love you.”
- No one-liners about rain.
- Drone remains polite; menace is policy.
- Maya never says she is sorry for the wipe. The recording accuses her of mercy. Her “No” is about whether the recorded man was sure — which is also about whether she is.

If a line can be a look, it is a look.
