# AFTERLIGHT Vertical Slice — Story & Character Bible

Status: **Design locked for Slice 01.** Not implemented in Unreal. Not full-game canon.

Working title of the slice: **Afterlight Window**

Target runtime: **8 minutes 15 seconds** (acceptable 7:30–9:00)

This document is the source of truth for character, world, and dramatic intent. Beats, screenplay, state graph, and production plans must not contradict it.

---

## 1. Central dramatic question

**Do I walk with Maya, or with the version of myself who already decided she was the danger?**

The slice must leave both sides alive:

1. Maya may be the only person who will keep him breathing tonight.
2. The man he used to be may have been right to stop her — or he may have been a coward leaving a recording in place of a conversation.

The player should exit wanting to know what these two people did together, not what “the corporation” is called.

---

## 2. What each person wants (this window only)

| | Wants now | Hides | Fear |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Eli** (unverified name) | To get out of the wet, to understand who is speaking to him, to not be written down by a camera | How badly the blanks scare him; he covers it with irritation | That he will follow the first competent person and become theirs |
| **Maya** | To get him into Pump House 12 before Helion’s Afterlight sweep finishes the underdeck | That she expected the wipe. That she is still executing a plan he can no longer consent to | That if he remembers the *work*, he will not come with her — and that if he never remembers, she is kidnapping a stranger who used to love her |

### Player belief

- **Start:** This woman found me. She knows my name. The city is hostile. Going with her is survival.
- **After the choice:** Either I decided she is safety, or I decided I will not be led.
- **End:** Survival and love may be the same trap. The past self is not a reliable narrator. Maya is not a simple liar.

## 3. Emotional arc (film rhythm)

The slice must **not** stay loud.

| Time | State | How it is felt |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:50 | Disorientation | Rain, body, no name. Almost no music. |
| 0:50–1:40 | Curiosity | A specific person, not a quest giver. |
| 1:40–2:25 | Fragile connection *or* early guard | The choice. Both are adult. |
| 2:25–3:10 | Uneasy companionship | Walk-and-talk. Space to look at the world. |
| 3:10–4:25 | Urgency | Helion lantern-drone. Hide. Maya is too practiced. |
| 4:25–5:10 | Apparent safety | Door, dry air, the city muffled. |
| 5:10–6:25 | Warmth | The quiet beat. The player should almost trust her. |
| 6:25–7:50 | Disturbance | The recording. Meaning of the warmth changes. |
| 7:50–8:15 | Unresolved pull | She does not beg. The city comes back. Title. |

Breathing room is mandatory in Underdeck observation, the walk through Lantern Cut before the drone, and the first thirty seconds inside the pump house.

## 4. Protagonist mini-bible (slice only)

**Working identity:** She calls him **Eli**. He does not confirm it. Production may label him Eli in dialogue scripts; the character does not introduce himself.

**Do not lock:** full name, age beyond “thirties,” occupation, crime, family.

### Immediate state

- Cold, rain-soaked, a dull ache behind the eyes, copper taste.
- Motor skills work. He is not staggering-drunk amnesia. He is a competent body with a missing filing cabinet.
- Clothes: dark work-adjacent layers, no obvious gang or corporate uniform. A cheap clip on his collar with no device attached (the recorder was removed).

### Personality (what still operates)

- Observational. He comments on physical facts, not feelings.
- Irritable when people manage him.
- Does not perform confusion. He hates the sentence “I don’t remember.” He never says it.
- Default social move: answer a question with a smaller question, or with action.

### What he knows

- Rain. Pain. A woman who used his name.
- That cameras in this place are not decoration.
- That his left/right habits disagree with his hands.

### What he does not know

- Whether Eli is his name.
- Why Maya is wet on purpose (she came back for him).
- That he agreed to a memory wipe.
- The work they did.
- Whether he loved her.

### Instinct / fear / weakness

- **Instinct:** Follow competence. Keep moving.
- **Fear:** Being catalogued. Being owned.
- **Weakness:** He wants the story of himself more than he wants to be safe — but he will not admit that until the recording forces it.

### Maya as he perceives her

A tired woman who talks as if they have a shared schedule. Attractive in the way of someone who has not slept; he does not have the language for that yet. She could be a handler, a partner, or both.

### Hidden contradiction (slice)

He asks to be told the truth and flinches when a hand moves too fast toward his things.

## 5. Maya mini-bible (slice only)

**Name in this slice:** Maya. No surname spoken.

**Do not lock:** family, exact age, whether she is “enhanced,” her job title.

### Apparent personality

- Dry. Short sentences. Not sultry, not quip-machine.
- Practical to the point of rudeness.
- Used to being the one who watches the time.
- Humor exists as a leftover: she starts jokes and abandons them.

### Internal state

She has been looking for him for hours. She is angry that the wipe worked as well as it did. She is ashamed of the anger. She is still going to finish getting him inside, because the alternative is Helion writing an unregistered adult in Underdeck 9 at 02:17.

She loves him. She will not say that. Love is not proof of innocence.

### Immediate objective

Pump House 12. Dry. Off the Witness map. Then, later (not in this slice), whatever “the rest” is.

### What she knows (slice-visible)

- His name as she believes it.
- The Afterlight window schedule.
- Helion lantern-drone cadence on this stack.
- Where the pump house key lives.
- That a tin exists which she hoped he would not find tonight.

### What she hides

- She expected him to forget her.
- She agreed to the wipe.
- She is not only rescuing him; she is continuing a plan the recorded man now calls “the rest.”
- She moved the recorder into the tin “for later.” That is a lie she tells herself. She hid it.

### Why she cares

Not “because the plot needs a girl.” Because this is the person she already burned a life beside. Abandoning the blank version would mean she only loved the useful one.

### What she fears he will remember

Not a password. A decision they made about other people. The slice must not specify the crime. The fear should read on her face when he picks up the tin.

### Sympathetic

- She came back into the rain.
- She knows how he holds a mug and is embarrassed when she gets it wrong.
- She does not plead after the recording. She lets him look at her.

### Suspicious

- She will not say the destination until he is already walking.
- She looks at his eyes too long, like a checklist.
- She is too good at hiding from civic drones.
- She reaches for the tin.

### Behavioral tells (use these, not a meter)

| Channel | High Trust (after follow) | High Suspicion (after question) |
|---|---|---|
| Distance | Close follow (~140 uu). She walks a half-step ahead, checks back. | Far follow. She stays off his shoulder. |
| Touch | Sleeve grab when the drone turns. | Open hand, no contact: “Down.” |
| Name | Uses “Eli” once more, quietly. | Stops using the name. |
| Joke | Completes the dishwasher line. | Swallows it. |
| Pump house | Sits on the floor near him. | Stays standing, towel on her own hair. |
| Recorder | “Don’t—” then she lets him. Shame. | “Eli—” too fast, a grab she aborts. |

She must be capable of feeling trustworthy and possibly dangerous in the same minute. The tells change *texture*, not the plot path.

## 6. World micro-bible (slice only)

**Place:** **Lowtide Stack**, Harbor Nine precinct. Reclaimed cargo platforms stacked into housing. When the tide gates close, runoff sheets off the underdecks. Civilians live above. Service people and unregistered people use the wet.

**When:** Near-future, not dated. Night. Civic clock in Lantern Cut reads **AFTERLIGHT 02:17**.

**Afterlight:** Local word for the nightly brownout window. Helion dims public lighting and thins Witness coverage to save grid and to herd people into registered shelter. It is not a secret. It is policy. Unregistered movement happens now because the cameras are “variable.”

**Helion:** Municipal security contractor. Bureaucratic, not neon-god. Their visible tech in this slice:

1. **Witness posts** — wall boxes with a small lens and a status LED. Dead red during thin coverage. Live white when writing faces.
2. **Lantern-drones** — slow civic quads with a polite recorded advisory, not a gunship.

**What civilians understand:** Afterlight means go home if you have a registered address. Don’t argue with lanterns. Don’t look into Witness if you can help it.

**One environmental story beat:** Laminated Helion notice, rain-warped: `AFTERLIGHT HOURS — REDUCE GRID — WITNESS COVERAGE VARIABLE`. People have underlinined VARIABLE in pen.

**Faction that may matter later:** Helion. Do not explain memory technology, neural anything, or a conspiracy name in this slice. If a system did the wipe, the recording is the only evidence, and it sounds like a man talking about a decision, not a product.

**Visual rule:** Sodium maintenance lamps, teal emergency strips, tide marks, wet concrete, one steaming kiosk. Avoid generic “cyberpunk wallpaper.” No Japanese-as-texture. No stolen city skylines.

## 7. Locations (three connected spaces)

Keep the slice spatially small. One greybox spine.

### A. Underdeck 9

- **Narrative:** He wakes. The world is wet and specific. Maya finds him here.
- **Visual:** Covered service corridor under a transit platform. Rain sheets in curtains at the open bays. Puddles. Pipe lagging. One Witness post with a dead LED.
- **Gameplay:** Look, stand, walk, first Talk.
- **Light:** Cool spill from the deck slots; sodium down the length. His face is half in rain-light.
- **Audio:** Rain on metal. Distant train. No score at first.
- **Camera:** Explore. First Dialogue OTS at Talk.
- **Story in the space:** Empty collar clip. A Witness post he can look at (Maya will dislike that).
- **Transition:** She leads him up a short stair into Lantern Cut.

### B. Lantern Cut

- **Narrative:** The city in miniature. Policy on the wall. The sweep. Maya’s competence shows.
- **Visual:** Narrow pedestrian cut between stacked housing. Shutters. One kiosk still steaming (broth, plastic stools chained). Civic clock. Helion notice. Lanterns are practical, not festival.
- **Gameplay:** Walk-and-talk (Full). Then hide in the kiosk / shutter recess (Constrained).
- **Light:** Warmer kiosk sodium vs cold street LEDs. Drone spotlight is a moving white circle.
- **Audio:** Afterlight quiet. Kiosk radio, very low. Drone voice is polite and wrong.
- **Camera:** Explore while walking. Threat when hiding. No combat camera.
- **Story in the space:** The notice. A child’s toy abandoned under a stool. The drone does not care about the toy.
- **Transition:** After the drone passes, a service door painted the same dirty teal as the emergency strips. Pump House 12.

### C. Pump House 12

- **Narrative:** Apparent safety. Human heat. The tin. The recording. The city returns.
- **Visual:** Decommissioned flood-control room. Gauges, a dead pump, personal life overlaid: two mugs, a coat on a hook in his size, a towel, a face-down photo, a drawer that doesn’t belong on a pump schematic.
- **Gameplay:** Enter, look, optional inspect photo, required inspect tin. Short Cinematic for playback.
- **Light:** One work lamp. Warm. Tight. Rain becomes muffled thunder on the hatch.
- **Audio:** Pump tick (even dead pumps settle). Then almost silence for the recording.
- **Camera:** Explore on entry. Intimate for the quiet beat. Reveal insert on the tin. Cinematic for playback. Intimate on Maya’s face after. Brief Threat-adjacent framing when the Witness LED outside dies up to live.
- **Story in the space:** Two lives were here. One of them prepared a message for a man who would not be him.
- **Transition:** Out of the slice — not out of the room. Title over black.

## 8. Canonical facts (do not contradict)

1. Eli does not confirm his name.
2. Maya came back into the rain for him.
3. Helion is civic security, not the named villain of the scene.
4. The wipe happened. He consented, at least on the recording.
5. Maya expected it and is still executing “the rest.”
6. The recording is his voice and his face (still + audio in first production).
7. The choice does not fork the locations or the reveal. It forks Maya’s texture.
8. No combat. No HUD in the intended YouTube capture besides optional dialogue captions.
9. Default relationship at slice start matches current systems: **Trust 0.50, Suspicion 0.00**.
10. Choice deltas match current lab magnitudes so thresholds already work: Follow → Trust **+0.25**; Question → Suspicion **+0.65**.
11. Spoken recording is locked in `vertical-slice-screenplay.md` (B09). Do not paraphrase it in implementation.

---

## 9. What this slice is not

- Not the origin of the entire conspiracy.
- Not a meet-cute.
- Not a boss fight.
- Not a tour of a city.
- Not a copy of any film in which a man is told not to trust a woman by a future/past self as the entire thesis. The AFTERLIGHT version is specific: he told himself that **her care is the remainder of a plan**, and that **wanting her in the room** means he has not finished becoming the man who made the recording. Love is the operational risk. That is ours.

---

## 10. Title

**AFTERLIGHT** on black. No tagline.

The word has already appeared as civic policy on the clock and the notice. The title should feel like the city said it first.
